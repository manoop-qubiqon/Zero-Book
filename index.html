FAQ

Questions, answered.

How can a laptop have no screen?

The Holographic Projection Unit renders your display in the air above the device. You choose its size and position, and it can split into several panels.

Do I ever need to charge it?

No. The Infinity Power Module keeps the device always powered — no chargers and no power banks.

How do I type?

A full-size keyboard is projected onto any flat surface. Gesture and voice input work alongside it, and an optional wearable adds tactile feedback.

Can I connect to a monitor or projector?

Yes. Seamless connectivity lets ZeroBook Infinity mirror or extend to any compatible device or display.

Is my data private?

Your data is encrypted on the device and stays yours.
